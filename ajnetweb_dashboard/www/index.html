How it works

  1. Design – pick a theme, language and which views the client gets.
  2. Rooms & Devices – hide rooms or entities and pin favourites.
  3. Support Info – your company contact, shown on the admin-only Installer view.
  4. Deploy – generates one responsive dashboard: phones get 1 column, tablets 2–4.

Your choices are saved in the add-on. Add new devices in Home Assistant, then deploy again – customisations are kept and the previous version is backed up automatically.

Licence

When AJ Netweb Remote Access is activated on this Home Assistant, every AJ Netweb add-on and the dashboard are licensed automatically. Sites without Remote Access: send the Install ID to AJ Netweb, paste the licence key you receive and press Activate.

Dashboard

Kiosk & menu

For wall tablets locked with a kiosk. A ⋯ More button at the end of the bottom bar gives people the pages that don't fit, their own theme & colour, and (for those you allow) settings. Each line decides who sees it. Use Specific people with Home Assistant user names (the display name, e.g. Mama, panel-majlis). Hiding Home Assistant's header / sidebar needs the kiosk-mode plugin (HACS) or Fully Kiosk's full-screen mode.

Theme

Native Home Assistant themes – no card-mod or HACS needed. Two-mode themes follow the device light/dark setting.

Views

Home and one page per room are always included. Views with no matching devices are skipped.

Rooms & Devices

Rooms come from Home Assistant Areas (grouped by Floor). Untick a room to hide it, untick a device to remove it everywhere, ★ to pin it on Home.
