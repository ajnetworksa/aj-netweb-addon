# AJ Netweb Prayer & Adhan

The adhan on your home speakers at every prayer time, built on Home Assistant's native
**Islamic Prayer Times** integration (offline calculation, Umm Al-Qura and 20+ methods).

- Plays on Music Assistant, Sonos, Google Cast, DLNA and any media player that can play a file.
  Music Assistant and "announce" speakers pause the music and resume it automatically.
- Pauses selected TVs during the adhan and resumes them after.
- Separate Fajr adhan, optional dua after the adhan, per-prayer on/off, offset and volume.
- Phone reminders before the adhan, Ramadan suhoor reminder, Hijri date.
- Dashboard controls for the whole family (on/off, mute next, play now, stop) and a prayer card
  in the AJ Netweb dashboard.
